### Zero Shot
- With zero-shot prompting you skip providing sample outputs to the LLM.
    - relies on the model's general knowledge
    - no examples provided
    - faster to create
    - more variability in output

 When to use zero-shot:
 1. Quick iteration — first drafts & quick ideas
 2. Broad summaries
 3. Marketing ideation
    - Creating general marketing concepts, taglines, content themes, etc.
 4. General writing tasks
    - Creating content without strict style requirements or brand voice.

When not to use it:
1. strict format requirements
2. Specific branding consistency (marketing)
3. Compliance-heavy workflows
4. Highly-technical Tasks
